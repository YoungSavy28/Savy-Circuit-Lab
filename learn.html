Add a part from the toolbar, then pick a red (+) or black (−) wire, then drag from one terminal (small circle) to another.

The circuit workbench uses battery-level DC. Box-wiring boards are de-energized diagrams, not AC power simulations or installation approval. Real building wiring can kill. Work on real circuits only with training, supervision, the supply isolated and locked out as applicable, and absence of voltage verified.
