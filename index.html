About

Learn it by wiring it.

Savy Circuit Lab is a hands-on electrical classroom. Each lesson is split into short parts. Parts start with a quick animated demo, then you drop parts on a workbench and run the wires yourself. Bulbs light up, current flows, and live volts and amps show you exactly what the circuit is doing, so series and parallel stop being words in a book.

1

Watch the demo

A short animation shows how a complete circuit works: source, load, and the loop back. Pause it, step through it, replay it.

2

Wire it yourself

Add batteries, bulbs, switches and junctions. Drag from one terminal to another to run a wire. Add as many bulbs as you want.

3

See what is happening

Current flows along the wires you drew. Turn on the numbers to read volts, amps and watts, and burn a bulb out on purpose to see why.

4

Check yourself

Every lesson has goals that check themselves as you build, plus a short quiz that explains the answer.
